Methodology
Institutional Risk Index (IRI) — 4 weighted dimensions. Every alert is explainable, not a black box.
35%
Financial Risk
Anomalies in budget execution, payment flows and AECID treasury operations
30%
Procurement Risk
XAI detection of irregular patterns in tenders and contracts (PLACE + BDNS + OCDS)
20%
Operational Risk
Institutional performance and management metrics of fund-receiving entities
15%
Data Risk
Quality, completeness and timeliness of publicly available information by source

The 3 Structural Ruptures
They explain why traceability collapses between link 3 and link 7.
R1
IOs — Black box
Funds to UNDP, UNICEF, FAO, UNHCR… that aggregate multi-donor contributions without breaking down the Spanish contribution in IATI. Traceability is cut at L3.
R2
Sub-contracting without OCDS
Contracts awarded without publication on the State Procurement Portal (PLACE) under the Open Contracting Data Standard.
R3
No auditable justification
Projects >€500,000 without a published final evaluation nor a favorable response to an information request (Transparency Law 19/2013).
Risk Indicators
Integrated score = 60% risk (ICR + SOG + RES + VIA) + 40% inverted traceability. Classification: GREEN / YELLOW / ORANGE / RED.
ICR
15%
Recipient Concentration Index
Normalized HHI. Detects whether a few actors concentrate most of the funds.
SOG
35%
Management Opacity Score
Weighted sum of binary indicators: is an IO, has R2, has R3, direct award, no declared country.
RES
30%
Cut-off Link Risk
Inverse of the traceability score. The lower the link reached, the higher the risk.
VIA
20%
Institutional Vulnerability
Proxy for the World Bank Governance Index (WGI 0-100) for the recipient country.
